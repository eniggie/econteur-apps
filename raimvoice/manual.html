RAIMVOICE

Owner’s Manual

Edition 1 · for app version 1.0.2

RAIMVOICE gives a small business a phone number answered around the clock by two AI agents, Eve and Adam. They talk to your customers, book real appointments on your calendar, and hand you every conversation in plain English.

This manual covers all of it, in order, with screenshots: setting up, the Today screen, the phone line, the inbox and AI drafts, the calendar, your customers, every setting, plans and usage, your customers’ privacy, what to do when something looks wrong, and how to reach a human.

Download the manual (PDF, 2.4 MB, 17 pages)

Two things the manual will not do

It will not claim the AI is a person. Eve and Adam identify themselves as AI at the start of every call, because US law requires it and because pretending otherwise would be the wrong product.

It will not invent facts. If a price, an opening time or a policy is not in the information you gave the app, the agent says it will have you confirm rather than guessing.
