Privacy Policy Terms of Use Support

Effective date: August 22, 2026

RAIMFUN is a free, offline collection of 88 casual games for iPhone and Android. No account, no ads, no internet connection required.

Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and OS version.

A game will not open

Close RAIMFUN completely and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart the device, then reinstall the app. Your best scores are stored on the device and are removed by a reinstall.

My best score disappeared

Best scores are stored only on the current device. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or moving to a new device without a matching backup will clear them.

Does RAIMFUN need the internet?

No. All 88 games run fully offline.

Does RAIMFUN have ads or purchases?

No. There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, and no subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Accessibility

Game boards, colour controls, and score elements carry accessibility labels, and completions are announced. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver or TalkBack, please tell us and we will fix it.
