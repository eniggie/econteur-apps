Privacy Policy Terms of Use Support

Effective date: August 22, 2026

RAIMFUN is a free collection of 33 thinking games for iPhone, iPad and Android. No account, no sign-in, no ads and no purchases. Every game plays offline; only the Champion Board needs a connection.

Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and OS version.

A game will not open

Close RAIMFUN completely and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart the device, then reinstall the app. Your best scores are stored on the device and are removed by a reinstall.

My best score disappeared

Your progress and best scores are stored only on the current device. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or moving to a new device without a matching backup will clear them.

Does RAIMFUN need the internet?

No. All 33 games play fully offline. The Champion Board needs a connection to post and show scores; scores you finish offline simply are not posted.

Does RAIMFUN have ads or purchases?

No. There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, and no subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Progress is stored only on your device: use Profile > Reset all progress, or delete the app. Scores posted to the Champion Board are removed with Profile > Remove my names. To stop posting, turn off Profile > Post my scores. See Delete your data.

Accessibility

Game boards, colour controls, and score elements carry accessibility labels, and completions are announced. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver or TalkBack, please tell us and we will fix it.
