RAIMFUN is a free game for everyone that keeps your mind working and your thinking sharp. It is published by ECONTEUR LLC and sponsored by RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

There is no sign-in, and no account (version 1.2.5 and later)

RAIMFUN has no sign-in, no sign-up, no email address and no password. Earlier versions offered sign-in with Apple or Google so a public name would belong to somebody; that has been removed, and the app no longer contains either company's sign-in software.

What stays on your device

Every game works with no account and no internet connection. Your progress, favourites, character and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. You can remove them by deleting the app or using Profile > Reset all progress.

Music by RAIMZY

The game music in Profile > Music is built into the app and plays without a connection. Newer versions of RAIMFUN can also play songs by RAIMZY, if you choose them there (they are off unless you do). Those songs are streamed from our apps website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages: the app downloads the song list and the songs, and, like any website, GitHub receives your device’s internet address in order to send them. These requests carry no nickname, identifier or account, and we receive no information from them. The Listen button opens the song’s song.link page in your browser or music app; what happens there is covered by those services’ own privacy policies.

The Champion Board

When you first open RAIMFUN you choose a nickname. While Profile > Post my scores is on (it is on unless you turn it off), each game you finish posts to that game’s Champion Board when you have a connection. Turn the switch off and nothing is posted; everything else keeps working.

  • What is posted: your nickname, the game, your score and the day. This is public: everyone using RAIMFUN can see it. Please do not use your full real name or anything that identifies you.
  • A random identifier: the first time you post, our server creates a random value and your device keeps it. It is not an account, it is not linked to you, Apple, Google or any email address, and it identifies nobody off your device. It exists so we can limit how many scores one device posts each day, attach a report to a name, and delete your names when you ask.
  • Reports: if someone reports a name, we store which entry was reported and the reason given, so a person can review it.

We use this information only to run the Champion Board, prevent abuse and review reports. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Champion Board data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. It is kept until you remove your names, or until we remove an entry that breaks the Terms of Use.

Removing your names

In the app, open Profile and press Remove my names. This immediately deletes every score that device posted to the Champion Board, the random identifier that owned them, and any reports about those names. There is no account to close. Progress on your device is left for you to keep or remove. You can also email us; see Delete your data.

Donations

The "Support the Foundation" button opens a donation page run by Stripe for RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc. in your web browser. RAIMFUN itself collects no payment information. Anything you enter on that page is handled under Stripe's privacy policy. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Age

RAIMFUN is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over, which is the target age group we declare on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has put a name on a Champion Board, contact us and we will remove it.

Permissions and third parties

RAIMFUN does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or advertising identifier. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Changes to this policy

If RAIMFUN's data practices change, this page and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
