Effective date: August 22, 2026

RAIMFUN is an offline collection of 88 casual games published by ECONTEUR LLC. The app is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, or personal-data collection.

Information we collect

RAIMFUN does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier.

Game progress

Your scores and game state are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your play experience. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to a third party. You can remove it by deleting the app or clearing its local data.

Children

RAIMFUN is rated for a general audience of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties and advertising

RAIMFUN contains no third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, social login, in-app purchases, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data deletion

Because no data leaves your device, deleting the app deletes everything RAIMFUN has stored about you. If you would like written confirmation that we hold no data about you, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If RAIMFUN's data practices change, this page and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
