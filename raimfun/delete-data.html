This page explains how to delete data associated with the RAIMFUN app, published by ECONTEUR LLC.

Delete everything from your device

  1. Delete the RAIMFUN app from your device.
  2. That is all that is required — RAIMFUN stores nothing outside your device.

Request deletion by email

To ask us to delete anything held about you, email developer@econteur.com with the subject “Delete my RAIMFUN data”. We confirm within 30 days. Because RAIMFUN uses no account, please include any detail that helps us locate a record — for example the approximate purchase date.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted: your best scores and local game state. These are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

Kept: nothing. RAIMFUN has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server. We hold no personal data about you at all, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Retention period: none — no data ever leaves your device.
