Where RaimAction stands out

  • Plays offline, no accountSolo play needs no connection and no sign-in. Call of Duty: Mobile's listing says an internet connection is required, and Epic says Fortnite is online-only.
  • No ads, no paid random itemsAll 11 games here carry Google Play's notice that in-game purchases include random items, and three carry the “Contains ads” label. RaimAction has neither.
  • 50 different games in one appEach operation is its own game: six are free forever, and a Premium operation is free each week.
  • TEAM UP by room codeShare a 4-digit code and a friend anywhere in the world joins. HARD is built for two.
  • Console feelPlayStation, Xbox and Backbone controllers on every screen, haptics and a Photo Mode.

Where others are stronger (honestly)

  • Scale and live contentThese games come from large studios, with very large player bases, seasonal events and competitive play. RaimAction is made by a small independent company.
  • Bigger squads and PvPTEAM UP is for two players. Many of these games run squads of four or more and player-versus-player matches.

Questions about RaimAction

Is RaimAction free?

Yes. RaimAction is free to download. Six operations (AEGIS, STRIKE, LAST STAND, RELIC RUN, DELTA and ECHO) are free forever, and one Premium operation is free to play each week. RaimAction Premium unlocks the other 44 operations, either as a yearly subscription that renews automatically or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app and vary by country.

Does RaimAction work offline?

Yes. You can play solo with no internet connection and no account. TEAM UP, the online two-player mode, and the champions boards need a connection.

Can I play RaimAction with a friend in another country?

Yes. With TEAM UP (online co-op), one player hosts a room and shares its 4-digit code, and a friend anywhere in the world joins. HARD is built for two players.

Is RaimAction on Android and iPhone?

Yes. RaimAction is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android phones and tablets. It plays in landscape.

Who made RaimAction?

RaimAction is created and designed by ECONTEUR LLC, founded by Dr. Ephraim Oviawe, who performs as RAIMZY, “The Heartbeatboy”. His songs are the game’s optional soundtrack. The free operations are sponsored by RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc.

Does RaimAction have ads or loot boxes?

No. There are no ads and no loot boxes, and nothing is pay-to-win: Premium unlocks operations, and Guardian Pass rewards are looks and merits only.

Can I play RaimAction with a controller?

Yes. PlayStation, Xbox and Backbone controllers work on every screen, with haptics.

What is RaimAction about?

RAIMACTION is a game about courage, not violence: face what you fear, keep your mind busy, and outthink the challenge in front of you.

Is it “RaimAction” or “Raim Action”?

The official name is RaimAction, and its star operator is written RAIMACTION. People also search for “Raim Action”, “RaimAction game” or “the RAIMZY game”, and phones sometimes autocorrect it to “rain action” or “reaction”. They all mean this game.

How is RaimAction different from PUBG Mobile or Call of Duty: Mobile?

Both are large online multiplayer games. On Google Play both carry the “Contains ads” label and a notice that in-game purchases include random items, and the Call of Duty: Mobile listing says an internet connection is required. RaimAction plays offline, has no ads and no loot boxes, and is built around 50 solo and TEAM UP operations. See the full, sourced comparison.
