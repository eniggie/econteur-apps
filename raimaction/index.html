RaimAction is about courage, not violence: face what you fear, keep your mind busy, and outthink the challenge in front of you.

One third-person action game with thirty-three operations — assault, sniper, stealth, heist, a three-phase boss, battle royale, hunting and more — for iPhone, iPad and Android tablets and phones.

  • Nine operators — meet each one in the Showroom and choose your weapon.
  • EASY, MEDIUM, HARD — none of them is a walkover.
  • Built for touch — left-handed controls, invert look, aim assist, auto fire.
  • Fully offline — no account, no ads, no in-app purchases. Nothing leaves your device.
