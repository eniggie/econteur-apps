RaimAction is an offline action game for iPhone, iPad and Android — thirty-three operations, nine operators, no account, no ads and no purchases.

Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and system version.

There is no sound

Open TOOLS (or pause a game) and check that SOUND is on and the volume is above zero. On iPhone, sound plays even with the silent switch on; turn the device volume up.

It is too easy, or too hard

Choose EASY, MEDIUM or HARD in TOOLS. Only the enemies change — the maps and objectives are the same, so every win counts.

Choosing an operator and a weapon

Tap OPERATOR on the main screen to open the Showroom. Drag to turn your operator, use the arrows to see the others, pick a weapon, and press SELECT. Sniper, hunt and stealth operations keep the weapon they were built around.

Controls feel wrong

TOOLS has look speed, invert look, left-handed controls, auto fire, screen shake and vibration. On older devices, turn HIGH DETAIL off for a smoother frame rate.

My progress disappeared

Progress lives only on your device. Deleting the app, or moving to a new device without a device backup, removes it.
