RaimAction: Neon City is an offline futuristic action game for iPhone, iPad, and Android with a 119-stage campaign and the Neon Brawl fighting mode. No account, no ads, no internet connection required to play.

Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and OS version.

Premium Operations did not unlock

Open Premium Operations and tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the approximate purchase date and the store you used.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Request refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play's order history. We cannot issue store refunds directly.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on the current device. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or moving to a new device without a matching backup will clear it. Premium Operations can always be restored from the store.

The game runs slowly

Open Settings inside the game and enable Reduced Motion or Simple Assist, which lowers effects and simultaneous controls. Closing other apps also helps on older devices.

Accessibility

RaimAction supports larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, and large touch targets from Settings, and all controls carry accessibility labels. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver or TalkBack, please tell us and we will fix it.
