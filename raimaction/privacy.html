Effective date: September 28, 2026

RaimAction is an offline, single-player action game for iPhone, iPad and Android. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting software. Optional premium packs are one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple or Google. The game itself sends nothing about you anywhere.

Purchases

If you buy an optional premium pack, the purchase is made and processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The game asks the store which packs you own and receives only that answer and the price shown; RaimAction never receives your name, email, card details or payment information, and we keep no purchase records of our own.

What is stored, and where

Your settings (look speed, volume and sound, difficulty, controls, operator and weapon choice) and your records (runs, wins and best score in each operation) are saved in a small file on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, device identifiers or advertising identifier. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we sell nothing.

Links that leave the app

Two buttons in TOOLS open your web browser: this privacy policy, and a donation page for RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc. run by Stripe. RaimAction itself handles no payment details; anything you enter on that page is handled under Stripe's privacy policy.

Donations go to RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc., a Georgia nonprofit corporation. It is not yet a registered 501(c)(3), so donations are not tax-deductible.

Children

RaimAction is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from them either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
