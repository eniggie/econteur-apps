RaimAction is an action game for iPhone, iPad and Android that plays offline. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting software. Optional premium packs are one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple or Google. The only thing the game ever sends to us is a run you choose to post on a champions board.

Champions boards (optional)

Every operation has a public board showing its top 10 and its champion. When you finish or win a run and choose to post it, RaimAction sends our board server:

  • your nickname (3–14 letters or numbers that you choose; please don't use your real name), shown publicly on the board;
  • your score and a short run summary, shown publicly;
  • a random install ID that the game makes up the first time you post. It is not your device's hardware ID or advertising ID, it is never shown to anyone, and it only lets the board keep one entry per player per operation.

This is not linked to your name, email or any account, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is never sold. Nicknames pass a word filter, any entry can be reported, any player can be blocked on your device, and entries reported by several players are hidden for review. Posting is optional; the game works the same without it. SHARE opens a champion card in your browser, which uses your device's own share sheet; we do not receive where you send it.

Playing together (co-op)

When you host or join a co-op room, the players' devices exchange each player's chosen operator, the name shown in the room and the moves of the run. In an online room (the default) this travels through our room server, which only passes it between the players in that room while the game is running; it is not stored and is never read for anything else. If the room server cannot be reached, the room runs on your Wi-Fi and the devices talk directly to each other. iPhone and iPad ask for Local Network access the first time a Wi-Fi room is used; single-player never needs it.

Purchases

If you buy an optional premium pack, the purchase is made and processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The game asks the store which packs you own and receives only that answer and the price shown; RaimAction never receives your name, email, card details or payment information, and we keep no purchase records of our own.

Music (optional)

If you choose the RAIMZY soundtrack, songs and their list are downloaded from our apps site (hosted by GitHub Pages) and kept on your device for next time. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any download does (your network address reaches the host). LISTEN opens the song on a music service of your choice, under that service's own policy.

What is stored, and where

Your settings (look speed, volume and sound, difficulty, controls, operator and weapon choice), your records (runs, wins and best score in each operation) and your board nickname are saved in a small file on your device. Apart from the board posts described above, they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, hardware device identifiers or advertising identifier. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we sell nothing.

Links that leave the app

Two buttons in TOOLS open your web browser: this privacy policy, and a donation page for RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc. run by Stripe. RaimAction itself handles no payment details; anything you enter on that page is handled under Stripe's privacy policy.

Donations go to RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc., a Georgia nonprofit corporation. It is not yet a registered 501(c)(3), so donations are not tax-deductible.

Children

RaimAction is not directed at children under 13. The champions boards collect no personal information beyond the nickname a player types, and we ask players not to use their real names.

Retention and deletion

A board entry is kept while the board exists, so it can be ranked. To have your entries deleted, follow Delete Your Data.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
