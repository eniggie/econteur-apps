RaimAction Premium and purchases

Some operations are free for everyone; the rest need RaimAction Premium. Players who installed RaimAction before Premium was introduced keep every operation that was free to them.

RaimAction Premium (yearly) unlocks every premium operation, lets you host any operation in co-op for friends in your room, and includes the Guardian Pass premium track, for one year. The price is shown in the app and on the store before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple or Google Play account when you confirm. It renews automatically every year at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current year; your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the year ends. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the end of the year you paid for.
