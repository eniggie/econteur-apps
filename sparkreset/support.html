SparkReset is a two-minute home-reset app for iPhone and Android.

Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and OS version.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on the current device. Clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or restoring a device without the relevant backup can remove it.

I do not receive reminders

Open SparkReset Settings and confirm Gentle reminder is enabled, then confirm notifications are allowed for SparkReset in your device's system settings.

How do I remove my data?

Open Settings and erase local progress, or uninstall SparkReset.

Does SparkReset need an account?

No. SparkReset works without an account.

Does SparkReset show ads?

No. SparkReset contains no advertising and no cross-app advertising trackers.

How do I restore Premium?

Open SparkReset Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Open SparkReset Settings and choose Manage subscription. Purchases are managed by the App Store or Google Play. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues through the end of the current paid period. See the Terms of Use for full renewal terms.
