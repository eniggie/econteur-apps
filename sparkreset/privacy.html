Privacy Policy Terms of Use Support

Effective date: August 22, 2026

SparkReset is designed to work without an account. Your reset history stays on your device. If you use SparkReset Premium, Apple or Google processes the payment and RevenueCat validates the purchase and subscription status.

Information stored on your device

SparkReset stores your completed resets, spark total, streak, saved resets, custom resets, badge progress, Night Reset history, and preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's features.

Purchases and subscription status

If you view or buy SparkReset Premium, Apple or Google processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, product and purchase history, receipt or purchase token, subscription status, last-seen time, device type, operating-system information, locale, and currency code as needed to validate purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and operate subscription analytics. SparkReset does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Information we do not collect

SparkReset does not require your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifier. It does not upload your reset history, show advertising, sell personal data, or use data for cross-app advertising tracking.

Notifications

If you enable Gentle reminder, SparkReset asks for notification permission and schedules one local notification on your device at the time you select. The reminder does not require a server and does not transmit your data. You can disable it inside SparkReset or in your device settings.

Sharing

If you choose "Share this tiny win", your device's system share sheet opens with a short message. SparkReset does not receive the destination or a copy of what you share. The app you choose may process the shared message under its own privacy policy.

Data deletion

You can erase SparkReset's local progress from Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain purchase records as required to provide purchases, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Email us for help with the anonymous RevenueCat customer record.

Children

SparkReset is a general-audience productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud accounts, advertising, attribution integrations, or other data collection, this policy and the app-store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
