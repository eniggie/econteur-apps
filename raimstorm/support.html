Contact us

Email developer@econteur.com. We answer within two business days. Please include your device model and OS version.

operations did not unlock

Open operations and tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the approximate purchase date and the store you used.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on the current device. Deleting the app, clearing its data, or moving to a new device without a matching backup will clear it. operations can always be restored from the store.

The game runs slowly

Open Settings inside the game and enable Reduced Motion or Simple Assist, which lowers effects and simultaneous controls. Closing other apps also helps on older devices.

Accessibility

RAIMSTORM supports larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, and large touch targets from Settings, and all controls carry accessibility labels. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver or TalkBack, please tell us and we will fix it.
