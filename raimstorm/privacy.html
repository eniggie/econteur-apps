Effective date: October 14, 2026

RAIMSTORM is a single-player storm-rescue game that works without an account. It plays offline. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a run you choose to post on a world board.

Information stored on your device

RAIMSTORM stores your stage progress, records and best scores, your chosen rescuer, purchases you own, the nickname you chose and your accessibility settings locally on your device. This is used only to run the game.

World boards (optional)

Every operation has a public world board showing its top 10 and its champion. When you finish or win a run and choose to post it, RAIMSTORM sends our board server:

  • your nickname (3–14 letters or numbers that you choose; please don't use your real name), which is shown publicly on the board;
  • your score and a short run summary (for example the stage reached), shown publicly;
  • a random install ID that the app makes up the first time you post. It is not your device's hardware ID or advertising ID, it is never shown to anyone, and it only lets the board keep one entry per player per operation.

This information is not linked to your name, email or any account, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is never sold. Nicknames pass a word filter, any entry can be reported, any player can be blocked on your device, and entries reported by several players are hidden for review. You can choose not to post; the game works the same without it. A champion card you share opens your device's own share sheet; we do not receive where you send it.

Music (optional)

If you choose the RAIMZY soundtrack, songs and their list are downloaded from our apps site (hosted by GitHub Pages) and kept on your device for next time. The request carries nothing about you beyond what any download does (your network address reaches the host). LISTEN opens the song on a music service of your choice, under that service's own policy.

Purchases

Premium rescues are one-time purchases made and processed entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. RAIMSTORM never receives your name, email or payment details.

Information we do not collect

RAIMSTORM does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier. It shows no advertising, contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

A board entry is kept while the board exists, so it can be ranked. To have your entries deleted, follow Delete Your Data. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device, subject to your device backup settings.

Children

RAIMSTORM is not directed at children under 13. The world board collects no personal information beyond the nickname a player types, and we ask players not to use their real names.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, advertising or any other data collection, this policy and the app-store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
