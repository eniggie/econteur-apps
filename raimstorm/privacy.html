Effective date: September 4, 2026

RAIMSTORM is an offline action game that works without an account. Your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

RAIMSTORM stores your stage progress, high scores, credits, awards, loadout choices, weapon mastery, training completion, and accessibility settings locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game and is never uploaded by us.

Information we do not collect

RAIMSTORM does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier. It shows no advertising, contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing

If you choose to share a stage milestone, your device's system share sheet opens with a short message. RAIMSTORM does not receive the destination or a copy of what you share.

Data deletion

You can erase local progress from Settings inside the game. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.See Delete Your Data.

Children

RAIMSTORM is rated for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, advertising, online play, or any other data collection, this policy and the app-store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
