Questions people ask

Who makes RAIMZEAL, V3EDGE, RaimCalls and Raimovia?

ECONTEUR LLC, a software company founded on 11 November 2011 by Dr. Ephraim Oviawe. Every product on this site, including GetIntoStores, RAIMFUN, RaimAction and SparkReset, is created and designed by ECONTEUR LLC.

Which ECONTEUR apps are free?

RAIMZEAL's Foundation plan is free forever. RAIMFUN is free with no ads or purchases. V3EDGE is free with no paid plans today. RaimAction has 35 free operations and optional packs, and SparkReset has a free version with optional Premium. RaimCalls and Raimovia are paid business tools; Raimovia starts with a 7-day trial.

Do ECONTEUR apps use Chinese AI?

No. No ECONTEUR product uses a Chinese AI model. Our AI features run on providers such as OpenAI, and apps like RaimCalls and V3EDGE ask for your consent before using them.

Is the RAIMZEAL Health Foundation a charity?

RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc. is a Georgia nonprofit corporation. It is not a registered charity and not a 501(c)(3): its application for federal tax exemption was filed in September 2026 and has not been granted. Donations to it are not tax-deductible.

When is V3EDGE coming to iPhone and Android?

There's no date yet. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are in progress, and we won't announce a date until they're approved. Today, V3EDGE works free in your browser at v3edge.app/app.

How do I delete my data?

Open the app's Delete your data page from Privacy, terms & support, or email developer@econteur.com from the address on your account. Apps that keep data only on your device, such as SparkReset, are cleared when you delete the app.

Which ECONTEUR apps are on iPhone today?

RAIMZEAL is live on the App Store. RaimCalls, GetIntoStores, RAIMFUN, RaimAction and SparkReset are waiting for App Store review, and all of them are already live on Google Play.

Is RAIMZEAL medical advice?

No. RAIMZEAL is a wellness app. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything; for medical questions, talk to a qualified health professional.

What's the difference between RaimCalls and Raimovia?

RaimCalls is the phone app: it makes calls for you and answers your business line. Raimovia is the web platform for the rest of your business: inbox, CRM, bookings, payments and automations. Raimovia itself doesn't handle phone calls.

Do ECONTEUR games have ads or loot boxes?

No. RAIMFUN, RaimAction and SparkReset contain no ads, and RaimAction has no loot boxes.

How do I contact ECONTEUR?

Email developer@econteur.com.
