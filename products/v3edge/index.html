V3EDGE

V3EDGE is a video editor for creators, free today with no paid plans and no watermark. Remove your background in one tap, caption and translate automatically, and change the scene, outfit or lighting with AI Edit. It runs in your browser now, with mobile apps on the way.

What it does

For creators who make short videos and want polished results without a subscription.

  • One-tap background removalCut yourself out of any clip, then fill the background with a colour, a blur or a photo.
  • Green screenKey out a green background for a clean composite.
  • A real timelineTrim, split, change speed from 0.25x to 4x, and crop to 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9.
  • 18 transitions and 15 effectsEnough to give every edit its own look, all included.
  • Auto-captions and lyricsGenerate captions or on-screen lyrics automatically, then tweak them.
  • Caption translationTranslate your captions into 26 languages.
  • AI voice-overAdd a narrated voice-over without recording one yourself.
  • AI EditDescribe a change, such as a new background, location or outfit, adding or removing something, or new lighting, on clips up to 60 seconds. V3EDGE asks your permission before sending a clip to an AI provider.
  • Free, with no watermarkNo paid plans, no in-app purchases and no watermark on your exports.

V3EDGE is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Today it runs in your browser, where background cut-out is processed on V3EDGE's own server.

AI Edit sends a clip to an outside AI provider only after you give permission. V3EDGE does not use Chinese AI models.

Questions about V3EDGE

Is V3EDGE free?

Yes. V3EDGE is free to use today, with no paid plans, no in-app purchases and no watermark.

When is V3EDGE coming to iPhone and Android?

There is no date yet. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are in progress, and we will only announce a date once they are actually approved. Until then, the full editor works in your browser at v3edge.app/app.

What is a good CapCut alternative without Chinese AI?

Adobe Premiere mobile and Edits by Instagram are free, US-owned and available now. V3EDGE uses only non-Chinese AI providers and asks before sending any clip; it runs in the browser today, with mobile apps coming.

What is the best free video editor with no watermark?

Adobe Premiere mobile and Edits both export without a watermark for free. V3EDGE is also free with no watermark and no paid plans.

How do I remove a video background for free?

In V3EDGE, open your clip and tap background removal; then fill the background with a colour, a blur or a photo. CapCut, InShot, VN, Premiere and Edits also offer one-tap cut-out.

Is there an AI that changes my outfit or lighting in a video?

Yes. V3EDGE's AI Edit takes a typed request, such as a new background, outfit or lighting, on clips up to 60 seconds. Premiere offers Firefly Generative Fill, but its listing does not describe outfit changes.

What is a cheaper alternative to the Captions app?

Captions' first paid tier is $24.99 a month. Free auto-caption options include V3EDGE, Premiere mobile, Edits and VN, though only Captions advertises 100+ caption languages.
