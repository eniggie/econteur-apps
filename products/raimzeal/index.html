RAIMZEAL

What it does

For anyone who wants one place to eat better, move more and understand their body, without paying for four separate apps.

  • Ovia AI chat, free every dayAsk Ovia AI about meals, training, sleep or your cycle. The free plan includes 10 chats a day; paid plans include more.
  • Barcode macro tracking, freeScan a barcode to log calories and macros in seconds. No upgrade needed.
  • Workouts on the free planFollow guided workouts at no cost. The AI Workout Generator, which builds sessions around you, comes with Rise.
  • Women's health, fully includedPeriod tracking, cycle sync, PCOS, pregnancy and menopause modes are all part of the free Foundation plan.
  • Sleep, water, fasting and calmTrack sleep, hydration and fasting windows, and take a breathing or mindfulness break when you need one.
  • A daily Foundations lessonOne short lesson a day to help healthy habits stick.
  • Community and challengesJoin challenges and keep each other going.
  • Apple Watch appRAIMZEAL comes with a companion app for Apple Watch.
  • AI meal scanner (Rise)Photograph your plate for an AI estimate of calories and macros. Part of Rise, $9.99 a month.
  • Talk to Ovia AI out loud (Reign)Real-time voice conversation with your coach: 90 minutes a month on Reign ($19.99/month) or 240 on Legacy ($49.99/month).

RAIMZEAL is a wellness app. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. For medical questions, talk to a qualified health professional.

Paid plans: Rise $9.99/month or $99/year, Reign $19.99/month or $199/year, Legacy $49.99/month or $499/year. Voice conversation and AI meal-photo scanning are paid features; daily Ovia AI chat is free.

How to get RAIMZEAL

  1. Step 1Download RAIMZEAL from the App Store or Google Play, or open raimzeal.com in your browser.
  2. Step 2Create your free account and tell Ovia AI what you want to work on.
  3. Step 3Use the free Foundation plan for as long as you like. Upgrade only if you want voice coaching or the AI meal scanner.

Questions about RAIMZEAL

Is RAIMZEAL really free?

Yes. The Foundation plan is free forever, not a trial. It includes daily Ovia AI chat (10 a day), barcode macro tracking, workouts, women's health trackers, sleep, hydration, fasting and mindfulness tools, the daily lesson and community challenges.

What do the paid plans add?

Rise ($9.99/month) adds the AI meal scanner, the AI Workout Generator and more chat. Reign ($19.99/month) adds real-time voice conversation with Ovia AI, 90 minutes a month. Legacy ($49.99/month) raises voice to 240 minutes a month.

Is there a free MyFitnessPal alternative with barcode scanning?

Yes. RAIMZEAL's free Foundation plan scans barcodes, and so does Cronometer's free tier. MyFitnessPal lists barcode scanning as a Premium feature.

What app tracks workouts, food and my period in one place?

RAIMZEAL does all three on its free plan, including PCOS, pregnancy and menopause modes. Google Health Premium also combines them, at $9.99 a month.

Can I talk to an AI fitness coach out loud?

Yes. RAIMZEAL's Reign plan ($19.99/month) includes real-time voice conversation with Ovia AI, 90 minutes a month.

What is a free alternative to Fitbod?

Fitbod has no permanent free tier; its App Store price is $15.99 a month. RAIMZEAL's free plan includes guided workouts, and its AI Workout Generator is part of Rise ($9.99/month).

Is RAIMZEAL a medical app?

No. RAIMZEAL is a wellness coach. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it is not a substitute for a qualified health professional.

Who makes RAIMZEAL?

RAIMZEAL is created and designed by ECONTEUR LLC, founded in 2011 by Dr. Ephraim Oviawe.
