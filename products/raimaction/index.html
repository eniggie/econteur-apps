RaimAction

RaimAction is a third-person action game about courage, not violence. Play 35 free operations on your own, even offline, or team up online with two to four players using a room code. There are no ads and no loot boxes.

What it does

For players who want tight action and co-op with friends, without ads or loot boxes.

  • 35 free operationsA full set of missions you can play without paying anything.
  • 16 operators, 9 weaponsPick the operator and loadout that suit your style.
  • Three difficulty levelsEASY, MEDIUM or HARD, so new and seasoned players both get a fair fight.
  • Online co-op for 2 to 4Share a room code and play together. Co-op needs an internet connection.
  • Solo play offlinePlay operations on your own with no connection at all.
  • Champions boardsCompare runs with other players, with report and block tools built in.
  • Optional RAIMZY soundtrackSwitch on music by RAIMZY, or turn it off.
  • Fair extrasFour premium packs at $4.49 each, or all of them for $8.89. No loot boxes and no ads.

Questions about RaimAction

Is RaimAction free?

Yes. The 35 operations are free. Four optional premium packs cost $4.49 each, or $8.89 for all of them, and there's no subscription.

Can I play RaimAction offline?

Yes, solo operations play offline. Co-op needs an internet connection.

How does co-op work?

One player creates a room and shares the code; two to four players can join online.

Does RaimAction have loot boxes or ads?

No. There are no loot boxes and no ads. Extras are clearly priced, one-time packs.

RaimAction or Call of Duty: Mobile?

Call of Duty: Mobile requires an internet connection and centres on competitive multiplayer. RaimAction focuses on solo and co-op operations, and solo play works offline.

Is RaimAction like Space Marshals?

Both are action games with campaign missions. Space Marshals 3 sells chapters at $4.99 each and lists no multiplayer; RaimAction adds online co-op.

Is RaimAction on iPhone?

Not yet. It is live on Google Play, and the iPhone and iPad version is waiting for App Store review.
