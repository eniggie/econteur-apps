RAIMFUN

RAIMFUN is a collection of 33 thinking games with big buttons, a relaxed mode with no clock, and layouts that fit any phone or tablet. It is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and it plays offline.

What it does

For kids, grandparents and everyone in between who want a calm, fair game to play.

  • 33 games in one appPuzzles, word games, memory and logic games, each with its own Champion Board.
  • Free, for realNo ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no tracking.
  • Plays offlineEvery game works without a connection. Only the Champion Board needs the internet.
  • No sign-inPick a nickname and play. No email, no password.
  • Relaxed modeTurn off the clock and play at your own pace.
  • Big, clear buttonsLarge controls that are easy to see and tap.
  • Works at any text sizeGames stay readable even with your device's largest text setting.
  • Fits every screenEvery game is laid out to fit phones and tablets.

RAIMFUN is made for fun. It makes no claims about improving memory, intelligence or health.

RAIMFUN is sponsored by RAIMZEAL Health Foundation, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity. Donations to it are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

How to get RAIMFUN

  1. Step 1Install RAIMFUN from Google Play. The iPhone and iPad app is waiting for App Store review.
  2. Step 2Choose a nickname. There's no account to create.
  3. Step 3Pick any of the 33 games, and switch on relaxed mode if you'd rather play without a clock.

Questions about RAIMFUN

Is RAIMFUN a free alternative to Lumosity?

RAIMFUN is a free collection of 33 thinking games with no ads, purchases or sign-in. Lumosity sells Premium plans priced from $11.99 to $89.00 on the App Store. RAIMFUN is for fun and makes no brain-training claims.

Is RAIMFUN really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Can I play RAIMFUN offline?

Yes. Every game plays without internet. Posting to or viewing the Champion Board needs a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. You choose a nickname; there's no email, password or social sign-in.

Is RAIMFUN good for seniors and kids?

It was designed with both in mind: big buttons, a relaxed mode with no clock, support for large text, and layouts that fit phones and tablets.

Does RAIMFUN improve memory or IQ?

RAIMFUN makes no medical or cognitive-improvement claims. It is a collection of games to enjoy.

RAIMFUN or Elevate?

Elevate offers 40+ skill games on a subscription, and its App Store listing shows advertising. RAIMFUN is free, with no ads and no account.
