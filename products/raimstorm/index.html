RAIMSTORM

RAIMSTORM is a 3D storm-rescue game built for two players, on one phone or two. Seven rescues are free for everyone, with more in Premium. There are no ads and no account. Play it in your browser at play.raimstorm.com or get it on Google Play.

What it does

For two people who want to play together, side by side or on two phones.

  • Rescue togetherTwo players pull people out of floods, avalanches, hailstorms and tornadoes.
  • One phone or twoPlay side by side on one phone, or each on your own.
  • Seven free rescuesFree for everyone, with more rescues in Premium.
  • No ads, no accountStart playing without signing up.

How to get RAIMSTORM

  1. Step 1Play in your browser at play.raimstorm.com, or install it from Google Play.
  2. Step 2Pick a rescue and play with a friend on one phone or two.
  3. Step 3Questions? Email developer@econteur.com.

Questions about RAIMSTORM

Is RAIMSTORM free?

Seven rescues are free for everyone. Premium unlocks the rest.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. RAIMSTORM is built for two players, on one phone or on two.

Is RAIMSTORM on iPhone?

Not on the App Store yet. Play it on Android or in your browser at play.raimstorm.com.
