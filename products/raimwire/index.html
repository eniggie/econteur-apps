Questions about RaimWire

Is RaimWire a bank?

No. RaimWire is not a bank, does not hold customer money and holds no financial licence.

Does money move in RaimWire?

Not today. Sending, cards, savings and loans are shown as coming soon, and the practice flow never moves real money.

How do I get RaimWire?

Open raimwire.com in your browser. You can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app; there is nothing to download from a store.
