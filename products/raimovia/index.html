Raimovia

Raimovia is an AI business operating platform for small businesses. Role-based AI employees work from knowledge you approve, alongside a unified inbox, CRM, bookings, quotes, invoices and automations, and a weekly report tells you how the business is doing.

What it does

For owner-operators and small teams, such as consultants, studios and local services, who want one workspace instead of a stack of subscriptions.

  • AI employeesRole-based AI assistants that answer from the knowledge you approve, so they stay on-message.
  • Website chatbotAnswer visitors on your site and turn questions into leads.
  • One inbox for every channelEmail, SMS and web chat in a single inbox.
  • CRM, pipeline and lead scoringSee every contact and deal, and know which leads to call back first.
  • Bookings and public booking pagesShare a booking page and let clients pick a time that suits you both.
  • Quotes, invoices and paymentsSend a quote, turn it into an invoice and get paid in the same place.
  • Automations and campaignsFollow up automatically and run campaigns without extra tools.
  • Weekly owner reportA weekly summary of how your business is doing, delivered to you.
  • Knowledge base and Ask Ovia AIKeep your answers in one place and ask Ovia AI about your business.
  • Reviews, client portal and white-labelCollect reviews, give clients their own portal, and present it all under your brand.

Raimovia does not answer or place phone calls. For AI phone calls, use RaimCalls, a separate ECONTEUR app.

Plans are billed monthly: Foundation $99, Rise $299 and Reign $799. Legacy ($2,499) is arranged directly. Foundation starts with a 7-day trial.

How to get Raimovia

  1. Step 1Go to raimovia.com and start the 7-day Foundation trial, or install the Android app from Google Play.
  2. Step 2Add your services, prices and approved knowledge so your AI employees answer accurately.
  3. Step 3Connect your inbox and booking page, then let the weekly report show you what's working.

Questions about Raimovia

Is Raimovia a HubSpot alternative for small businesses?

Yes, for owners who want leads, quotes, payments, bookings and follow-ups in one AI-assisted workspace. HubSpot has a free CRM for up to two users and a much larger ecosystem; Raimovia is narrower and built for owner-operators.

Raimovia or GoHighLevel: which is simpler?

GoHighLevel is built for agencies, starting at $97 a month, and its AI Employee is a $50 to $97 add-on per sub-account. Raimovia is built for a single business, with AI employees included in the plan.

Does Raimovia answer phone calls?

No. Raimovia handles your inbox, CRM, bookings, payments, automations and reports. Phone calls are handled by RaimCalls, a separate ECONTEUR app.

Can Raimovia send quotes and take payments?

Yes. Quotes, invoices and payments are part of every plan's core workflow.

Is there a Raimovia iPhone app?

Not on the App Store today. Raimovia runs in any browser at raimovia.com, and there is an Android app on Google Play.

Does Raimovia send a weekly business report?

Yes. Owners receive a weekly report summarising how the business is doing. None of the competitor pricing pages we reviewed list an equivalent.

Raimovia or Jobber for a business that isn't a trade?

Jobber is designed for home-service trades with crews and dispatch. Raimovia is general-purpose, so it suits consultants, studios and local services that don't send out crews.

How much does Raimovia cost?

Foundation is $99 a month with a 7-day trial, Rise is $299 and Reign is $799. Legacy, at $2,499 a month, is arranged directly.
