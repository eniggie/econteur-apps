RIOSOVIA

RIOSOVIA is a pan-African money app in testing. It lets you send money between users, practice investing, take short money lessons and quizzes, and ask the Ovia AI money coach. Everything runs in sandbox mode today, so no real money moves.

What it does

Built first for people in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and South Africa.

  • Send money between usersTransfers between RIOSOVIA users, running in sandbox mode during testing.
  • Practice investingBuild a practice portfolio and learn how investing works, with no real money at stake.
  • Money lessons and quizzesShort lessons with quizzes to build everyday money skills.
  • Ovia AI money coachAsk questions about budgeting, saving and the app itself.
  • Africa firstDesigned for Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and South Africa from the start.
  • Established payment partnersPlanned payment rails are Paystack and Stripe.

RIOSOVIA is in sandbox testing. No real money is sent, held or invested, and RIOSOVIA is not yet licensed to provide financial services.

Nothing in RIOSOVIA, including the Ovia AI money coach, is financial or investment advice.

Questions about RIOSOVIA

Can I download RIOSOVIA?

Not yet. RIOSOVIA is in internal testing and isn't in the App Store or on Google Play. There's no release date yet.

Does RIOSOVIA move real money?

No. Everything runs in sandbox mode today, and RIOSOVIA is not yet licensed to provide financial services.

Which countries is RIOSOVIA for?

It is built first for Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and South Africa.

Can I invest with RIOSOVIA?

Only in practice. The investing feature is a sandbox for learning; no real money is invested.

Which payment providers will RIOSOVIA use?

The planned payment rails are Paystack and Stripe.

Does RIOSOVIA give financial advice?

No. The lessons and the Ovia AI money coach are educational and are not financial or investment advice.
