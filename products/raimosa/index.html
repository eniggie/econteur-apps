Questions about RAIMOSA

What is RAIMOSA?

A local desktop AI commander, also called Sentinel. It plans a task, asks for your approval, carries it out and writes a tamper-evident receipt.

Does RAIMOSA send my data to the cloud?

No. RAIMOSA runs locally, with no cloud and no telemetry.

Which computers will RAIMOSA run on?

macOS, Windows and Linux.
