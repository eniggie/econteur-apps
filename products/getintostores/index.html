GetIntoStores

GetIntoStores is a store for DRM-free ebooks and digital products from independent creators, with built-in readers for PDF, EPUB, audio and video. It opens with 45 ebooks by Dr. Ephraim Oviawe, priced from $6.69 to $10.99, each with a free sample.

What it does

For readers who want to buy direct from authors, and for creators who want their own store without building one.

  • DRM-free downloadsWhat you buy isn't locked to one app or device.
  • 45 ebooks by Dr. Ephraim OviawePriced at $6.69, $8.89, $9.99 or $10.99.
  • Free samplesRead the opening of a book before you decide.
  • Readers built inRead PDF and EPUB, and play audio and video, without leaving the app.
  • Storefronts for creatorsCreators get their own storefront and a seller workflow. GetIntoStores keeps a 10% commission on sales.
  • Discount codes and affiliatesRun a sale with a code, or let affiliates share your books.
  • ReviewsRead what other buyers thought, and leave your own.
  • Gifts and bundles on the webSend a book as a gift or buy a bundle at getintostores.com.
  • Easy sign-in, easy exitSign in with Apple or Google, and delete your account whenever you choose.

Gifts and bundles are available on getintostores.com only, not inside the iPhone or Android apps.

How to get GetIntoStores

  1. Step 1Open getintostores.com or install the Android app. The iPhone app is waiting for App Store review.
  2. Step 2Browse the books and read a free sample of any title.
  3. Step 3Sign in with Apple or Google, buy, and read in the built-in reader.

Questions about GetIntoStores

Why buy from GetIntoStores instead of Amazon?

You buy directly from the creator, the files are DRM-free, and every book has a free sample. Amazon offers far more reach and Kindle Unlimited, and pays authors a 70% royalty only on ebooks priced $2.99 to $12.99.

How much are the ebooks?

The 45 ebooks by Dr. Ephraim Oviawe cost $6.69, $8.89, $9.99 or $10.99. Discount codes and web bundles can lower the price.

Can I read a sample first?

Yes. Every one of the 45 ebooks has a free sample.

Can I gift an ebook?

Yes, on getintostores.com. Gifts and bundles aren't available inside the iPhone or Android apps.

What formats can I read?

The built-in readers handle PDF and EPUB, and play audio and video products.

Can I sell my own books on GetIntoStores?

Yes. Creators get a storefront and seller workflow, with discount codes and affiliates. GetIntoStores keeps a 10% commission on each sale.

GetIntoStores or Gumroad for creators?

Gumroad charges 10% plus $0.50 per direct sale and 30% on sales through its Discover marketplace. GetIntoStores keeps 10% and includes built-in readers for your buyers.
